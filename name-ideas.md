# Name Ideas for the AI Blocker Extension

## Top Picks

### 1. **Clearview**
Clean, human, implies clarity and an unfiltered view. Easy to say and remember. Not obviously "AI blocker" which is a plus — less chance of platform retaliation from AI companies.

### 2. **Unplug**
Simple, punchy, emotionally resonant. "Unplug from AI." Works as a verb and a brand. Strong TikTok energy — "I unplugged my browser from AI for a week."

### 3. **Purist**
Appeals to the identity of the target user. "I'm a purist — I use Purist." Implies quality, intentionality, and rejection of artificial. Premium-sounding.

### 4. **RealFilter**
Functional and direct. Clear value prop in the name. "Filter for real content." Works well for SEO since people search "real vs AI images."

### 5. **HumanMode**
Very strong brand story. "Switch to Human Mode." Could anchor a whole "anti-AI lifestyle" brand. Strong TikTok concept — "I went Human Mode for 30 days."

### 6. **NativeWeb**
Implies the "original," unaugmented internet. Slightly more technical but interesting. Positions AI as an intrusion on the native experience.

### 7. **Organic Browser** / **Organify**
Borrows the "organic" food framing that already resonates with health-conscious consumers. "Organic content, no AI additives."

### 8. **Signal**
Short, powerful, implies truth-cutting-through-noise. Already used by the messaging app though — trademark risk.

### 9. **Antidote**
AI is the "disease," this is the cure. Dramatic but memorable. "The antidote to AI-generated everything."

### 10. **Basecamp** (concept, not the brand)
The idea of "base" — unaugmented, foundational, human. Could use "Basewall," "Baseweb," etc.

## Naming Advice

- Check trademark databases (USPTO.gov) and domain availability before committing.
- Avoid putting "AI" in the name — Chrome Web Store may deprioritize extensions with "AI" in the title that don't themselves provide AI features.
- Short names (1–2 syllables) work better in TikTok audio: "I installed Unplug" vs "I installed the AI Content Blocker Extension."
- The name should work as a verb if possible — "I Puristed my browser" / "Just Unplug it."
